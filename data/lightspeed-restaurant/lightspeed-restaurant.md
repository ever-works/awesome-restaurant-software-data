## Overview

Lightspeed Restaurant is a cloud-based point of sale and restaurant management platform built for hospitality businesses of all sizes, from single-location restaurants to multi-location groups and hotel restaurants.

## Key Features

- Cloud-based POS with offline mode (data syncs automatically when connection is restored)
- Customizable menus, screens, and floor plans
- Tableside POS with handheld devices
- QR code ordering and online ordering
- Multi-location management with unified guest data and reporting
- Hotel PMS integration for charge-to-room functionality
- Inventory management tools
- Payroll and workforce management including scheduling, labor forecasting, and tip management
- Advanced reporting and business intelligence
- Embedded payment processor (Lightspeed Payments) with flat-rate pricing
- Partner API access and integrations with third-party tools (e.g., 7shifts, OpenTable)
- 24/7 phone and chat support

## Pricing

Pricing varies by subscription tier. Lightspeed offers Starter, Essential, and Premium plans. Specific pricing is quote-based and depends on the number of locations and operational requirements.

## Deployment

Cloud-based with local offline capability.
